# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately to **team@darkwallet.cash**. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it and the impact you expect. We will acknowledge your
report, keep you informed while we fix it, and credit you in the published fix if you wish.

Contact details are also published at
[darkwallet.cash/.well-known/security.txt](https://darkwallet.cash/.well-known/security.txt).
